# Methodology

The platform will follow a staged methodology:

1. Preserve raw source Excel workbooks unchanged.
2. Load workbook and sheet metadata.
3. Normalize fields into canonical representations.
4. Validate source records and produce quality reports.
5. Generate master datasets.
6. Join reference datasets such as GI products and scoring weights.
7. Produce analytics and export intelligence outputs.
8. Generate reports and recommendations.

Sprint 1 implements only the foundation needed for this workflow.
